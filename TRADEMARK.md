# The PropMesh name and logo

The licenses ([`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md)) cover the code, the board
designs and the docs. They do not cover the **PropMesh** name or logo,
which identify the official project and the boards made by it.

**Without asking, you may:**

- Say your project or product is "based on PropMesh", "PropMesh-compatible"
  or "runs PropMesh firmware" when that's true.
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, videos, forum posts
  and talks.
- Build and sell boards from these designs — under your own name.

**Please ask first before you:**

- Call a board, kit, product or fork "PropMesh", or put the logo on it.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the project made, endorsed
  or supports your product.

Clones and variations are welcome; the name is how people tell the
official ones apart. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
