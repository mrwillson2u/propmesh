# PropMesh Pro v0.1 — pre-route design audit (2026-09-08)

**Scope.** All eight schematic sheets (read from KiCad's own netlist export, not
from the sheet docs), every symbol → footprint pin mapping, every footprint
against the drawing for the sourced LCSC part, datasheet conformance for every
active device, and the placed-but-unrouted board (243 footprints, 0 tracks,
0 zones).

**Method.** `kicad-cli sch erc`, `kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity`,
`tools/checknet.py` × 8, `tools/checksetup.py`, geometry scripts over the saved
board (pad-to-pad distances, courtyard overlaps, keep-outs), and a datasheet
pass per block against the manufacturers' own documents. **The sheet docs were
treated as claims to test, not as evidence.** Where a doc claim was checked it
is marked ✅ confirmed or ❌ wrong below.

**Tool baseline as saved:** ERC 3 errors (one part — §1.1). DRC: 2 shorts,
2 mask bridges, 1 courtyard overlap, 1 library mismatch, 12 drill-size errors
(one footprint), 9 parity warnings; the 499 unconnected items are expected with
nothing routed. `checknet.py` green on sheets 01 and 03–08, red on 02.
`checksetup.py` green.

Severity: **BLOCKER** = wrong board if built · **HIGH** = functional defect or
datasheet violation · **MEDIUM** = fix before v0.2 / at layout · **LOW** = nit.

## 1. Fix before routing

### 1.1 BLOCKER — the EN reset capacitor `C1` is disconnected (sheet 02)
`C1` (1 µF, the ESP32-S3 power-on-reset delay) was dragged on the MCU sheet in
the uncommitted edit — `mcu+usb.kicad_sch` moves it from (170.18, 76.2) to
(168.91, 53.34) and deletes the junction at (170.18, 72.39). Its two wires did
not follow. ERC reports `C1` pin 1, `C1` pin 2 and its GND symbol unconnected;
schematic parity reports the board still has `C1` on `Net-(U2-EN)`/`GND` while
the schematic says otherwise. Espressif requires the RC on EN for a clean
power-on reset.
**Fix:** re-wire `C1` pin 1 to the EN net (`R3` / `SW1` / `U2` pin 3) and pin 2
to GND. Re-run ERC and `checknet 02`.

### 1.2 BLOCKER — both DRV8871 ILIM pins sit on one net (sheet 06)
`/Motors/MA_ILIM` has four members: `U7.ILIM`, `U8.ILIM`, `R22`, `R24`.
`motors.kicad_sch` carries the label **`MA_ILIM` twice**, at (182.88, 102.87)
and (182.88, 67.31); the second should read `MB_ILIM`.

TI's DRV8871 datasheet (§7.3.4, Eq. 1) gives I_TRIP = 64000 / R_ILIM with
R_ILIM in Ω, minimum 15 kΩ, and its own worked example in §9.2.1 uses
**R_ILIM = 30 kΩ → I_TRIP = 2.1 A** — so the sheet's 30 k / 2.13 A choice is
✅ correct in isolation. As drawn, though, each driver sees the two 30 kΩ in
parallel (15 kΩ, right at the legal minimum, ≈4.3 A), and the two chips' ILIM
references are tied together, so neither channel has a defined trip point and
the two motors are coupled.
**Fix:** rename the second label `MB_ILIM`; re-run ERC and `checknet 06`.

**Why the checker missed it.** `checknet.py` maps each spec net to the schematic
net with the most matching members, then lets every spec net claim pins from a
shared pool — so two spec nets that collapsed into one schematic net both report
"ok" with no extras. A scan of all eight yamls for this pattern found
`MA_ILIM`/`MB_ILIM` as the only real instance. Worth one assertion: distinct
spec nets must resolve to distinct schematic nets.

### 1.3 HIGH — channel numbering does not follow the connector order (sheet 03)
- **Servo headers**, left to right on the board `J2 J3 J4 J5 J6 J7`, carry
  `SERVO_1 2 3 4` **`6 5`** — `SERVO_5` is on `J7`, `SERVO_6` on `J6`.
- **DC outputs**, top to bottom down the left edge `J8 J9 J10 J11 J12 J13`,
  carry `LED1_K` **`LED3_K LED2_K`** `LED4_K LED5_K LED6_K`. The FET/flyback
  pairs were then placed by number, so `Q2`/`D3` sit beside `J9` but switch
  `J10`, and `Q3`/`D4` sit beside `J10` but switch `J9` — two 2 A returns would
  have to cross.

`checknet` cannot see this: all six terminals share one Value.
**Fix (schematic):** swap `SERVO_5`↔`SERVO_6` and `LED2_K`↔`LED3_K`, re-run
`checknet 03`, F8 to the board.

### 1.4 HIGH — the digital-input clamp is on the wrong side of its series resistor (sheet 07)
Netlist: `DI1_RAW` = `J41.3`, `J46.3`, `D12.COM`, `R40.1` — the BAT54S clamp is
at the **connector**, and the 1 kΩ is between the clamp and the GPIO.

The BAT54S orientation itself is ✅ correct (Diodes DS11005: BAT54S is the
series pair; pin 1 anode → GND, pin 2 cathode → +3V3, pin 3 common → signal,
which clamps the signal to −Vf / 3V3+Vf as intended). The problem is the order.
With the clamp first, the diode carries the **full** fault current, limited only
by whatever is driving the pin:

- `J46` is a bare 0.1″ PIR header wired `5V | GND | SIG` in parallel with input 1.
  A dupont plug fitted backwards puts the board's own **+5 V on SIG**, and the
  upper clamp diode then conducts 5 V straight into the +3V3 rail with nothing
  limiting it. The rail is dragged toward ~4.4 V; the ESP32-S3's absolute
  maximum is 3.6 V. That takes out the MCU, the card, the PCA9685 and the DAC.
- A 12 V miswire into a sensor XH does the same, harder.

Put the 1 kΩ first and the clamp on the GPIO side and the same mistakes become
8 mA and 12 mA — nothing. (Clamp-at-the-port is the better arrangement for pure
ESD, but this board's realistic threat is a sustained miswire, and the
consequences are not comparable.)
**Fix:** on sheet 07, move `R40`/`R47`/`R52` to the connector side and
`D12`/`D13`/`D14` to the GPIO side, for all three inputs.

### 1.5 HIGH — test points short live nets (board)
DRC: `TP25` (STATUS_LED, B.Cu) lands on `C43` pin 2 (GND); `TP31`
(MOTOR_A_IN1) lands on `J6` pin 3 (SERVO_6). Both also raise rear solder-mask
bridge errors. Near-misses against the ≥2 mm rule in `sheets/08`: `TP11` 0.7 mm
from `J44`'s GND pin, `TP30` 1.3 mm from `J26` pin 2, `TP3` 1.9 mm from `J40`
pin 3. Many pads are now far from the net they serve (`TP31` 53 mm, `TP3`
39 mm, `TP25` 39 mm, `TP6`–`TP9` 26–36 mm) because `placetps.py` ran against
the pre-edit placement.
**Fix:** once placement is final, re-run `tools/placetps.py` with the PCB editor
closed, then DRC.

### 1.6 MEDIUM — `F1` on the board is the stale import
DRC: `Footprint '01530008Z' does not match copy in library 'propmesh'`, plus
"annular width 0" and "padstack questionable" on the centre hole. The library
copy has the Ø3.81 board-lock peg as NPTH; the board still carries the original
plated version whose pad equals its drill.
**Fix:** select `F1` → right-click → *Update Footprint…*. Clears three DRC items.

### 1.7 MEDIUM — the fused input net has no name and no width class
`F1` pin 2 → `Q7` drain is `Net-(Q7-D)`. The `VIN_*` HighCurrent pattern does
not match it, so this 10 A segment would route at the Default 0.25 mm.
`layout.md` A20 step 2 already calls for a `VIN_FUSED` label; it is not in the
netlist (0 occurrences).
**Fix:** add the local label on sheet 01, F8, confirm the net lands in
HighCurrent.

### 1.8 MEDIUM — `J44`/`J45` courtyards overlap
Digital-in 2 and 3 overlap by ~0.2 mm; `J41`/`J44` and `D14`/`J45` touch.
Spread the five top-edge XH connectors by ~0.5 mm each.

### 1.9 LOW — the four mounting holes are unannotated (`REF**`)
Parity reports them as extra/duplicate footprints. Give them `H1`–`H4`.

## 2. The buck loops are not loops yet

Diodes' AP64350 datasheet §PCB Layout is explicit: *"2. Place the input
capacitors as closely across VIN and GND as possible. 3. Place the inductor as
close to SW as possible. 4. Place the output capacitors as close to GND as
possible. 5. Place the feedback components as close to FB as possible."*
TI's TLV62569 §10.1 says the same and adds *"the sense traces connected to FB
are signal traces — avoid noise being coupled."*

Measured on the saved board, pad to pad:

| Path | Now | Wanted |
|---|---|---|
| `U5` SW (8) → `L1` pin 1 | **10.8 mm** | ≤ 3 mm — this is the switch node |
| `L1` pin 2 → `C9` / `C10` | **21.6 / 17.0 mm** | ≤ 3 mm |
| `U5` BST (1) → `C6` | **11.3 mm** | ≤ 3 mm |
| `U5` VIN → `C7` / `C12` | 3.7 / 7.2 mm | ≤ 3 mm both |
| `L1` body → `R14` (FB top) / `C8` | 3.6 / 3.8 mm | ≥ 5 mm, far side of the IC |
| `U6` SW → `L2` pin 1 | 7.7 mm | ≤ 3 mm |
| `L2` pin 2 → `C16` | 13.1 mm | ≤ 3 mm |
| `U6` FB → `R19` / `R21` | **20.0 / 20.7 mm** | at the pin |

`C9`/`C10` currently sit on the **opposite side of `U5` from `L1`**, so the 5 V
output current would loop back across the IC. `R19`/`R21` are 20 mm away, up in
the 5 V buck's area, on a high-impedance sense node.
**Fix:** rebuild both blocks per `placement-power.csv`, or any arrangement
obeying the four datasheet rules above.

Rest of the power block is sound: `Q7`→`F1`→`J17`/`J18` and `C11` are compact.
`D9` (TVS) is 22 mm from `Q7`'s source in the bottom-left corner — it works on
the pour but belongs beside `C11`. `J49` carries the whole 3 A rail through a
2.54 mm shunt (rated ~3 A): fine for v0.1 bring-up, replace with a wide link or
0 Ω 2512 for production.

## 4. Noise separation

- `R29`/`R30`/`C32`/`C33` — the line-out RC on `LINE_L/R` — are **5.9–7.7 mm
  from `U5`** and 10.7–14 mm from `L1`. `layout.md` B4 asks ≥ 10 mm, and this is
  the one net on the board where the failure mode is audible. They are also
  20 mm from `CN1`, and TI's PCM5102A §12.1 wants the output filter at the
  connector. Move all four to `CN1`.
- `U9`, `U10`, `CN1`, the beads, both ADC dividers, USB and the digital inputs
  are all ≥ 17 mm from any switcher or motor part — good.
- **The LED series resistors are fine where they are.** `R34`–`R37` sit 4 mm
  from `U11` and 36–44 mm from the terminals. A series resistor on a driven line
  is a *source* termination and belongs at the driver; `sheets/05`'s "at the
  connector" rule is the thing to correct, not the board.

## 5. Mechanical, orientation, access

- **7 mm mounting-hole keep-out** (CLAUDE.md) is violated by `C1 C3 R3`
  (5.5–5.8 mm), `D9 D10 D11 R18 R20` (4.7–6.8 mm), `J45 J47 D14` (4.2–5.9 mm)
  and `J23` (4.7 mm). A 0603 at 5.5 mm clears an M3 head; **`J23` (a screw
  terminal at the bottom-right hole) and `J45` are the two to move** — a
  screwdriver and a standoff want the same space.
- **`U2` vs `J1`.** The module's USB pins are 13/14 on its **left** edge at
  (16.25, 17.6–18.9); `J1` and `U1` are to its right, so the D+/D− pair would
  run ~40 mm around or under the module. `layout.md` A9 already proposes the
  fix (connector left of the module, ESD in the path, 12 mm run). Either apply
  `placement-mcu.csv`, or rotate `U2` 180° and accept the U.FL pigtail exiting
  inboard.
- **Status pixel `LED1` is at (65, 50)** — mid-board, 2.7 mm above socket 1's
  module outline, so a plugged-in driver with a heatsink hides it. Spec §2.3
  wants it on the top edge facing the lid. Move it with `C44` and `R38`.
- **Rail LEDs `D10`/`D11`** are in the bottom-left corner behind the barrel
  jack, invisible once wired. They exist for staged bring-up — put them on the
  top edge.
- **Sockets:** rows are exactly **12.700 mm** apart, pin 1 to pin 1, both
  sockets, parallel ✅. Module-to-module gap 4.8 mm. But `R25` (the UART 1 kΩ)
  lies **between socket 1's rows, under the plugged-in driver** — unreachable,
  and it is one of the parts most likely to want a bodge. Move it out. `J50`
  (0.7 mm), `L2` and `C18`/`C19` (1.7 mm) sit against the module outlines: fine
  for height, tight for fingers.
- `Card1`'s courtyard extends 0.3 mm past the top edge (intended — the slot
  protrudes); `J1` is flush. Every screw terminal, XH, the jack, USB and the SD
  slot face outward across their own edge ✅.
- `U2` pad 41 carries eleven 0.2 mm thermal vias from KiCad's own footprint
  against a 0.3 mm minimum-drill rule → 12 DRC errors. JLCPCB does 0.2 mm on
  4-layer; either relax the constraint to 0.2 or edit the footprint copy.

### ❌ Doc claims that turned out wrong

**a) `sheets/04-audio.md`: the SD_MODE resistor window is stated as 324–763 kΩ.**
The upper bound is right; the lower bound is not. Staying below the B1 minimum
(0.65 V) with the pulldown at its 108 kΩ maximum needs
R > 108 k × (3.3 − 0.65)/0.65 = **440 kΩ**. The stated 324 kΩ would allow a
substitution — 390 kΩ, say — that lands at 0.72 V and silently selects **Right
channel only**, which is the exact failure the note was written to prevent.
620 kΩ as fitted is safe. **Fix the number in the doc to 440–763 kΩ.**

**b) `sheets/02` and `pinmap.yaml`: "GPIO 3 … internal pulldown holds the
default at reset".** ESP32-S3 datasheet Table 3-1 lists GPIO3 as **Floating**,
with no default bit value and no internal pull. The pin is harmless in practice
because its strapping function (JTAG source select) only applies once the
`JTAG_SEL_ENABLE` eFuse is burned — but the stated reason is wrong, and the
design's own answer is already on the board: `R53`, the DNP pad on that net.
**Recommendation: populate `R53` (10 k) by default.** It costs one 0603, makes
GPIO3 deterministic at reset, and does not affect the shifter input it drives.

**c) `sheets/01-power.md` still flags the `L2` footprint as 13 % undersized.**
The library now has 2.35 × 3.50 pads at ±3.025 — the datasheet value. Stale ⚠;
delete it.

### Findings that are real but not errors

**MEDIUM — `Q1`–`Q6` (AO3400A, 30 V) switch a rail specified to 28 V.** With
the flyback diodes the switched node is clamped to rail + Vf, so ~28.5 V worst
case against a 30 V part: inside the rating, with no derating left. And the
input TVS does not help — SMAJ33A clamps at **53.3 V**, far above these FETs,
so a genuine input surge takes out all six before the TVS conducts meaningfully.
The same is true for `Q7` at 40 V. This is a deliberate, defensible v0.1 trade
(the TVS was sized to protect the 63 V/100 V capacitors), but it should be
written down as such. A 40–60 V SOT-23 N-FET is a free footprint-compatible
swap for v0.2.

**MEDIUM — an A4988 or DRV8825 will not run until firmware drives the UART TX
pin high.** Positions 5 and 6 of the socket are tied together and land on
`TMC_UART_RX`, which reaches GPIO18 (an input) and GPIO17 through the 1 kΩ. On
an A4988/DRV8825 those positions are RESET and SLEEP, which must be **high** to
run. At reset GPIO17 is an input, so the net floats and the driver sits in
reset — a silent "the prop doesn't move". Adding an **unpopulated 10 k pull-up
pad to +3V3** on that net now costs nothing, matches spec §2.6's own
"unpopulated pull-up pads near boot-sensitive pins" rule, and is benign for the
TMC2209 (which drives PDN_UART push-pull and has its own internal pulldown).
Also worth a line in the firmware notes.

**LOW — `L1` is 6.8 µH where Table 1 specifies 5.5 µH.** The sheet's ripple
analysis justifying it is sound (25–35 % across 12–30 V). Note only that the
COMP values copied from Table 1 were characterised at 5.5 µH; the resulting LC
pole moves from ~10 kHz to ~12 kHz, which is a smaller change than the output
caps' DC-bias derating already causes. No action.

**LOW — HK4100F pin pitch.** The drawing gives column spacings 2.54 and
10.16 mm (12.70 overall); the imported footprint has 2.44 and 10.16 (12.60).
0.1 mm inside a 1.1 mm hole — absorbed, but it is a hint that the import was
traced rather than dimensioned.

**LOW — TF-01A pad width.** The drawing calls for 9 pads of 0.70 × 1.30 at
1.10 mm pitch; the footprint has 0.60 × 1.30 at 1.10. Pitch, count and the two
Ø1.00 NPTH pegs at 8.00 mm all match ✅; the pads are 0.1 mm narrow.

## 7. The stepper socket — the central claim, verified

`sheets/06` rests on one assertion: that positions 5 and 6 (an A4988's RESET and
SLEEP) can be hardwired together because an A4988 needs them tied and a TMC2209
puts PDN_UART there. **This is correct.** From Watterott's own
`SilentStepStick-TMC2209_v20.sch` (the reference design the whole ecosystem
clones), the logic header `J1` is:

| A4988 position | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A4988 name | EN | MS1 | MS2 | MS3 | RESET | SLEEP | STEP | DIR |
| SilentStepStick | EN | MS1 | MS2 | **SPREAD** | **JMP pad** | **JMP pad** | STEP | DIR |

`PDN_UART` is the **middle pad of a 3-pad solder jumper**, whose two outer pads
are exactly positions 5 and 6. The user bridges it one way or the other, so
tying both on the motherboard reaches PDN_UART either way — and because the two
positions are the *ends* of that jumper, never both live, tying them shorts
nothing. `DIAG` and `INDEX` are on a **separate 2-pin header `J3`**, which is
precisely why the aux headers `J21`/`J26` exist ✅.

Also confirmed from Watterott: **MS1 = address bit 0, MS2 = bit 1**;
**SPREAD low = stealthChop**; MS1/MS2 = GND/GND → 1/8 and VIO/GND → 1/32 in
standalone. So the silkscreen defaults in `sheets/06` (socket 1 GND/GND/GND =
address 0; socket 2 3V3/GND/GND = address 1, both stealthChop) are ✅ correct,
including the standalone microstep values quoted. `J2.7` = **VIO** (3.3–5 V),
fed from +3V3 ✅. Power row order `VM, GND, OA2, OA1, OB1, OB2, VIO, GND` puts
one coil on positions 3–4 and the other on 5–6, matching the XH order ✅.

One consequence worth documenting: on a **DRV8825** carrier that same VIO
position is `FAULT`, an open-drain output behind an internal series resistor.
Pololu states connecting it to a logic supply is safe, so feeding +3V3 is fine —
it simply means no fault readback, and ~2 mA out of 3V3 while a fault is
asserted.

## 8. Documentation and tooling drift

- **The BOM export is nearly empty of part numbers.** Only 19 symbols (the
  imported ones) carry an `LCSC Part` field; the other ~220 — every stock-symbol
  R, C, IC and connector — have none. `layout.md` A25 says the JLCPCB plugin
  "reads the `LCSC Part` field the sheet specs put on every symbol", which is not
  true today. The numbers exist in the `.net.yaml` files; a short script can push
  `lcsc:` into the symbols using the same Value pairing `checknet` already does.
- `checknet.py` has two blind spots worth one assertion each: merged nets (§1.2)
  and same-Value connectors in the wrong order (§1.3).
- `layout.md` Part A's coordinate tables describe the 2026-09-07 packer output,
  not the board as it now stands (`U11` is at (52, 48), not (55, 79); the motor
  terminals moved to the right edge; the coil XHs to the bottom edge). Regenerate
  them from the board or mark them historical.
- Value strings are inconsistent (`10U`, `10u`, `10u/16V`, `620K`, `470`).
  Harmless to `checknet`, untidy in a fab BOM.
