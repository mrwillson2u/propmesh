05-led — wiring

Read each diagram left to right along the signal path. Boxes are parts, rounded nodes are nets, the label on a line is the pin. A part's power-rail pins are written inside its box. A multi-unit chip (a quad gate) appears once per unit — one box per triangle, exactly as KiCad places it.
