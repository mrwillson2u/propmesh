The **Yard** section shows every board PropMesh can hear, two ways — switch with **Topology | Map**.

## Topology: is my mesh healthy?

Boards are arranged by how many hops they are from the bridge, with every radio link labeled by its signal strength:

| Line | Signal |
|---|---|
| green | strong (−60 dBm or better) |
| gray | ok |
| amber, dashed | weak (−75 dBm or worse) |
| red | lost |

The badge at the top sums it up: **HEALTHY**, **WEAK LINK** or **DEGRADED**. Click a board for its details: what it hears, its uptime, memory, battery and firmware.

## Map: where is everything?

The map is a drawing of your yard with each board placed where it really is. A weak link suddenly makes sense when you can see it crosses the house.

**Draw the yard** with the tools on the left:

| Tool | For |
|---|---|
| **Area** | the house, garage, beds, lawn — click each corner, click the first corner again to close |
| **Walkway** | paths and driveways — click along it, double-click to finish |
| **Border** | the property line, a fence |
| **Prop** | clip art — tombstones, skeletons, spiders, pumpkins — to mark where things stand |
| **Select** | move things; drag an edge to push it out; double-click an edge to add a corner, double-click a corner to remove it |
| **Erase** | click something to remove it |

Pick a shape with **Select** to label it and change its color. Scroll to zoom, drag empty ground to pan, and **⛶** fits everything in view.

**Place your boards** by dragging them from the **Not on the map yet** bar onto the map. Click a placed board and pick a picture under **DRAWN AS** — the board then shows on the map as a skeleton, a ghost, a cauldron…

<Aside title="Saved on the bridge">
The map is stored on the board the console is connected to, so your laptop and your phone see the same drawing.
</Aside>
