A **sequence** is a short, timed script: *at 0 s the eyes light, at 0.2 s the jaw opens, at 1 s the floods go red.* One sequence can use outputs on as many boards as you like, and they all move together.

## Making one

In **Sequences**, press **+ New sequence** and give it a name. Then press **+ Add first track…**. A track is one output on one board: a servo, a strip, a relay, a flood light. Add a track for each thing that moves.

There are two ways to put steps on a track. Use whichever suits you.

- **On the timeline**: double-click a track to add a keyframe, and drag it to move it. Drag the ruler to scrub through time. **−**, **+** and **Fit** zoom.
- **In the step table** below it: press **+ Add step**, type the time in **AT (s)**, pick the output and set the **VALUE**.

**GET THERE OVER** is how long the output takes to reach that value, and the shape of the move: **ease in & out**, **gentle start & stop**, **linear**, **ease in**, **ease out** or **soft landing**. Zero means jump straight there. On the timeline, press **R** on a keyframe to switch between a ramp and a jump.

<Aside type="tip" title="See it as you build it">
**Live preview** is on by default. It moves servos, LEDs, strips and floods as you edit, so you can watch the prop while you set the timing. Relays and motors only move when you press **Test** on a step, so a pump doesn't fire while you drag keyframes.
</Aside>

## Saving and playing

**Save to boards** sends each board its part of the sequence. From then on the sequence lives on the boards. If a board is off the mesh when you save, it gets its part later: the bridge keeps a copy and passes it on when the board comes back.

**▶ Play** runs the sequence once, everywhere. It is available once the sequence is saved and the show isn't **Off**.

## Ambient loops

Turn on **Ambient loop** and set **every … s** for things that should run on their own all evening: a flickering window, a slowly breathing glow, fog every few minutes. Ambient loops play whenever the show is **Ambient** or **Armed**. See [Running the show](/propmesh/show/running/).
