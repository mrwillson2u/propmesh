Inexpensive color flood lights that are normally controlled by the **BRMesh** phone app can join your show. Your prop boards talk to them over Bluetooth, so the floods can wash the house in orange at dusk and flash red when a scare goes off.

## Pairing lights

<Steps>

1. In **Nodes & Channels**, open the **✺ Floods** chip.

2. Under **SENDING BOARDS**, tick the boards that should talk to the lights. Pick the ones closest to the lights, and preferably more than one: every ticked board sends, so if one board goes down the lights keep working. The bridge never sends to lights. Press **Save to boards**.

3. Put the floods near one of the ticked boards and switch them **off**.

4. Press **Find lights…**, then **Search (8 s)**, then switch the floods **on**. The lights only announce themselves for a few seconds after they get power.

5. Press **Pair** beside each light that appears. You will see **✓ paired as light #1**, #2 and so on.

</Steps>

You don't need the app or a key. The first pairing creates a key and the boards keep it. A light that already belongs to the BRMesh app shows **paired elsewhere**. **Take over** moves it to PropMesh, and the app loses it. If a light won't take over, power-cycle it a few times to reset it and search again.

## Using them

Each light gets a row with a name, a color picker, a warm-white slider and **Off**. In [sequences](/propmesh/show/sequences/), a flood is a track like any other output, so a scare can turn the porch red for three seconds and back.

<Aside title="The lights never answer">
BRMesh only goes one way: the boards send, and the lights don't reply. The colors the console shows are what the boards **last sent**. If a light misses a message because it was out of range or unplugged, the console can't tell. Keep a sending board within a few meters of the lights.
</Aside>

## Limits

- Up to 255 lights, and up to 16 per sending board.
- A board on **battery** sleeps between messages, so it reacts late and sends nothing while asleep. Prefer mains-powered boards as senders. The console asks before you tick a battery board.
