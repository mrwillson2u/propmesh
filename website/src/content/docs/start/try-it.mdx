The console has a built-in **simulator**: a pretend bridge with two prop boards behind it. Everything works the way it does with real boards — assign pins, build a sequence, wire a trigger, draw your yard — and nothing you do leaves your browser.

<LinkButton href="/propmesh/console/?sim=1" icon="external" iconPlacement="end">Open the console</LinkButton>

## Things to try

- **Yard** shows the pretend boards and the radio links between them. Switch to **Map** and draw your yard with the tools on the left — drop a tombstone or two.
- **Nodes & Channels** lists every board. Pick one and give a pin a job — a servo, an LED strip, a sensor — then **Save to node** and drag the test slider.
- **Sequences** → **+ New sequence**, then **+ Add first track…** for the output you just set up. Double-click the track to add a keyframe, and drag it around — **Live preview** plays it as you edit.
- **Show** → drag a **⊙ Sensor** block onto the graph, drag a **⚡ Play sequence** block next to it, and connect the dots left to right. Press **▶ sim** on the sensor block to run the chain.

The simulated boards flip their first input every few seconds, so a sensor trigger will fire on its own.

<Aside title="Your work stays in this browser">
The simulator keeps its boards' settings in your browser's storage. Clear your site data to start over.
</Aside>
