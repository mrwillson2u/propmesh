## What you can build

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Props that react" icon="rocket">
		A motion sensor sees someone coming; a skull's eyes light up, a coffin lid rattles and the fog machine kicks on — on different boards, in sync.

		LED strips with a flickering fire effect, dimmable lanterns, and cheap Bluetooth flood lights — all part of the same show.

	<Card title="A show that runs itself" icon="seti:clock">
		Set it to come on at dusk and switch off at bedtime. One big **STOP ALL** button when you need it.

	<Card title="No wires between props" icon="random">
		Boards pass messages along to each other, so the far corner of the yard works even where your WiFi doesn't reach.

## What you need

- **ESP32 boards** — about $5–10 each. [Which one?](/propmesh/boards/)
- **A computer with Chrome or Edge** to set things up. Everything happens in a web page; there's nothing to install.
- Whatever your props already use: servos, LED strips, relays, motion sensors.

No soldering is required to get started, and no programming at all.
